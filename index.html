About

I’m Joshua, a frontend developer who enjoys turning ideas into clean, responsive websites and interactive web experiences.

My path into development started from the other side of software. I previously worked in technical and application support, troubleshooting production systems, investigating bugs, and helping users solve real technical problems.

That experience still influences how I build today. I care about understanding how things work, finding the source of problems, and creating solutions that I can confidently maintain and improve.

These days, I’m focused on building with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript while continuing to expand my full-stack capabilities.
